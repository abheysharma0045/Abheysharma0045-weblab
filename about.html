About GCET Jammu

Government College of Engineering & Technology, Jammu was established in 1993. It is situated at Chak Bhalwal, Jammu and provides engineering education in different branches.

Vision

To emerge as a pioneer centre of research and technology and contribute to nation-building through technical education, professional ethics and innovation.

Mission

To provide an atmosphere that supports students' educational success and develops technical skills, human values, leadership, creativity and innovation.
