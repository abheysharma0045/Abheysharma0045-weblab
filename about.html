About GCET Jammu

History, vision, mission and important information

Introduction

Government College of Engineering and Technology (GCET), Jammu was established by the Government of Jammu and Kashmir in 1993 with AICTE approval and is affiliated with the University of Jammu. The campus is spread over about 869 kanals at Chak Bhalwal, approximately 22 km from Jammu city.

GCET has autonomous status effective from academic session 2024-25 and is recognized by the UGC under Sections 2(f) and 12(b). The institute offers undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programmes.

Vision

To emerge as a pioneer centre of research and technology and contribute to nation-building through technical education, professional ethics and innovation.

Mission

  • Support students' educational success.
  • Develop technical skills and human values.
  • Encourage leadership, creativity and innovation.
  • Promote entrepreneurship among learners.

Key Features

  • Autonomous status from 2024-25
  • Five B.Tech disciplines
  • State-of-the-art laboratories
  • Training & Placement activities
