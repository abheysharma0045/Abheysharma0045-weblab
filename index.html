Welcome to GCET Jammu

Learning • Innovation • Engineering

Government College of Engineering and Technology (GCET), Jammu was established by the Government of Jammu and Kashmir in 1993 with AICTE approval and is affiliated with the University of Jammu. The institute now has autonomous status effective from the academic session 2024-25.

The college offers B.Tech programmes in Electronics & Communication, Electrical, Computer Science & Engineering, Mechanical and Civil Engineering, along with selected M.Tech programmes.
